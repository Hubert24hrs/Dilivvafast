Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Dilivvafast account and the personal data linked to it. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dilivvafast and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. If you have a delivery in progress, finish or cancel it first. Investor accounts with active bikes or pending withdrawals are closed through support.

Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email dilivvafastsupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and photo.
  • Saved addresses, notifications and saved payment-method labels.
  • Support chat history.
  • Courier documents (licence, ID, vehicle photo) and payout bank details.
  • Your sign-in. You are signed out on every device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Delivery records, ratings and payment/payout records, for up to 7 years, for accounting, tax and dispute handling. They are no longer linked to your name.

Any remaining wallet balance is forfeited when the account is deleted.
