Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Dilivvafast Technologies ("Dilivvafast", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Dilivvafast mobile delivery application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal data when you use our Service. By using Dilivvafast, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Full name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Location data: Real-time GPS coordinates are collected while the app is in use (and in the background for active drivers) to enable delivery tracking and courier matching.
  • Device information: Device type, OS version, push notification token, and app version for service delivery and diagnostics.
  • Payment information: Wallet top-up amounts and transaction history. Card details are processed directly by Paystack and are never stored on our servers.
  • Profile photos and documents: Optionally uploaded for account personalisation or driver onboarding.
  • Usage data: Delivery history, in-app activity, and crash reports collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Dilivvafast account.
  • To match customers with available courier drivers.
  • To provide real-time delivery tracking.
  • To process payments and maintain wallet balances.
  • To send push notifications about order status updates, promotions, and account alerts.
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

3. Location Data

Location data is the core of our delivery service. For customers, we collect foreground location to display the delivery map and show nearby couriers. For drivers, background location is collected during active delivery sessions to provide real-time tracking to customers. Location data is not sold to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We share personal data only in the following circumstances:

  • Delivery matching: Your pickup/drop-off address and name are shared with the assigned courier driver.
  • Service providers: Firebase (Google), Paystack, and Mapbox receive data necessary to provide their respective services (hosting, payments, maps).
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. Delivery records are retained for 7 years for accounting and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile and personal data from our systems.

6. Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure, which employs industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Access to Firestore data is governed by strict security rules that restrict each user to their own data. Passwords are never stored in plaintext — authentication is managed entirely by Firebase Authentication.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Object to or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability where technically feasible.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@dilivvafast.ng.

8. Children's Privacy

Dilivvafast is not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, database, analytics, and cloud functions.
  • Paystack — Payment processing.
  • Mapbox — Map rendering and geocoding.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of Dilivvafast after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
